EchoNotes privacy policy

Effective from 9 November 2026

EchoNotes is an Android and Wear OS app that records meetings and, when the user asks, creates transcripts, notes and minutes with Google's Gemini AI. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it is kept and who it is shared with.

In short: recordings and results stay on your phone. There is no EchoNotes server and no user account. Audio and text leave the device only when you ask to process a recording or ask a question. They are then sent to Google with your own Gemini API key.

1. Who is responsible

Dário Soares — dariotsoares+echonotes@gmail.com

2. Data the app handles

  • Meeting audio, recorded on the phone or the watch, or imported by the user.
  • Results: transcripts, summaries, decisions, action items, minutes, and the questions asked about each meeting with their answers.
  • Details entered by the user: title, participants, speaker names, a glossary of names and terms, and meeting types.
  • The user's Gemini API key, stored only on the device and encrypted with the Android Keystore.
  • AI usage counts (number of tokens), used to show the estimated cost.

The app does not collect location, contacts or advertising identifiers. It uses no analytics and no advertising.

3. Where the data is kept

Everything is stored on the phone, in the app's private storage. EchoNotes has no servers and sends no data to the person responsible for the app.

On the watch, each recording is kept until the phone confirms it has received it. The watch then deletes its copy. Transfers between the watch and the phone go directly over the two devices' Wear OS connection.

4. Sharing with Google (Gemini API)

When you ask to process a recording or ask a question about a meeting, the app sends the audio or the transcript to the Gemini API, with the context it needs: title, participants, glossary and meeting type. The connection is encrypted (HTTPS) and uses the user's own key. EchoNotes does not provide keys or pay for the service. Google processes this data under the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Each user chooses whether to use a free-tier or a paid-tier key. Under Google's terms as of this date:

  • In the European Economic Area, Switzerland and the United Kingdom, Google applies the paid-tier data terms to all requests, including free-tier ones. It does not use the content to improve its products.
  • Elsewhere, on the free tier, Google may use the submitted content and responses to improve its products. Human reviewers may read that content after it has been disconnected from the account. Google itself advises against sending personal or confidential information on the free tier.
  • On the paid tier, Google does not use the content to improve its products. It keeps it for a limited period, only to detect abuse and for legal reasons.

Uploaded audio files are deleted automatically by Google after 48 hours. For meetings with personal data outside the European Economic Area, we recommend the paid tier.

5. Sharing chosen by the user

The app sends data to other apps only when the user asks it to. This happens when the user shares notes or minutes (text or DOCX), saves a document to a folder of their choice, or creates a calendar event from an action's deadline. EchoNotes does not read the calendar or any other data on the device.

6. Permissions

  • Microphone: to record meetings, only when the user starts a recording.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to keep recording or processing while the screen is locked, and to show progress.
  • Internet: only to communicate with the Gemini API.
  • Network state, start at boot and keep awake: to resume an interrupted processing job by itself once there is a network connection.
  • Vibration (watch): to confirm that recording has started or stopped.

7. Retention and deletion

Data stays on the device until the user deletes it. Deleting a recording in the app also deletes its audio, results, ticked action items and questions. Uninstalling the app deletes all its data. Documents exported to the user's own folders stay where they were saved.

8. Security

Communication with Google is encrypted (HTTPS). The API key is stored encrypted with an Android Keystore key. All other data is kept in the app's private storage, which Android protects.

9. Recording other people

Recording a meeting means processing other people's voices and words. Whoever records is responsible for informing the participants and, where the law requires it, obtaining their consent. In the European Union, this includes the GDPR.

10. Age

EchoNotes is intended for people aged 18 or over, as the Gemini API terms require.

11. Your rights

Because the data stays on your device, you can view, correct and delete it directly in the app. For any question about this policy, contact dariotsoares+echonotes@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Portugal, the CNPD). Requests about data processed by Google should be made to Google.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with its new effective date. Important changes are also mentioned in the app's Google Play listing.
